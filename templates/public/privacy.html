Privacy Policy

How ShambaSense handles account details, farm records and messages you provide.

AT A GLANCE

We use information you submit to operate your account, organise farm records and provide platform assessments.

Contact us about your data →

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, ShambaSense stores details such as your name, username, email address, phone number and account role. A profile picture may be stored if you choose to upload one.

Farm records can include farm and field names, county, sub-county, ward, village, location descriptions, acreage, soil type and notes. Planting records can include crop and variety, planting dates, area, seed source, planting method, intended plant population, status and notes.

Inspection records can include crop stage and condition, soil moisture, weed pressure, visible pest or disease signs, plant and emergence counts, observations, actions taken and follow-up dates. Messages submitted through the contact form include the name, email, subject and message you enter.

ShambaSense does not automatically collect GPS coordinates through the farm forms.

2. How information is used

We use submitted information to operate farmer accounts, display and organise farm records, generate assessments and recommendations from recorded information, respond to enquiries and maintain the service.

3. Farm and agricultural records

Farm, crop, planting and inspection records are associated with the relevant account and used by ShambaSense features. They may contain location descriptions or operational information that you choose to enter. Do not include information you do not want stored in your workspace.

4. Information sharing

ShambaSense does not sell farmers’ personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to host, maintain or deliver the platform, and may be disclosed where required by applicable law. Access should be limited to what is needed to operate the service.

5. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the deployment to help protect stored information. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain farm records and meet operational or legal requirements. Retention and deletion requests are handled subject to applicable law and records that must be retained.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your account. Contact ShambaSense using the contact page to submit a request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

This policy is intended to be read consistently with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations. It is not a statement of legal certification or a substitute for legal advice.

8. Cookies and technical information

The authenticated platform uses Django session and security-related cookies to support sign-in, session management and form protection. Depending on the hosting environment, server logs may also contain basic technical request information used for troubleshooting and security.
