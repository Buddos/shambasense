# ShambaSense

ShambaSense is a Django web application for recording farm and crop activity and turning field observations into practical, traceable guidance. Farmers can manage their farms and fields, track crop plantings, record inspections, review establishment and season-timing assessments, and follow recommendations.

Assessments use recorded farm, variety, planting, and inspection data. In particular, season assessments compare a variety's recorded maturity range with the expected season-end date; they do not predict weather, yield, or actual harvest dates. Guidance is intended to support—not replace—local agronomic advice.

## Features

- User accounts with farmer, agronomist, and administrator roles
- Farm and field records
- Crop and variety records, including expected maturity ranges
- Planting records and crop lifecycle tracking
- Field inspections for crop stage, condition, soil moisture, weeds, and pest or disease signs
- Rule-based establishment and remaining-season assessments
- Prioritized recommendations with status tracking
- Dashboard summaries and PDF reports
- Public home, about, privacy, terms, and contact pages
